recognition, the absent internet permission and the five scripts are all verified.
Do not loosen them without checking the app still earns them.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Abhyas turns a page of your own notes into flashcards you can actually study.

Photograph a page. Abhyas reads the text on your device, finds the definitions,
the questions and the facts worth remembering, and proposes cards from them. You
keep the ones you want and drop the rest — nothing reaches your deck without you
agreeing to it first.

Then it schedules them. Abhyas uses FSRS, the modern spaced-repetition algorithm,
which learns how your memory behaves for each card rather than applying one fixed
formula to all of them. Every grade button tells you exactly how long pressing it
will put the card away for, so you are making an informed choice instead of a
blind one.

NOTHING LEAVES YOUR PHONE

Abhyas has no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It does not
request internet permission at all, which is not a promise but a fact Android
enforces — you can check it yourself in the app's permissions. The text
recognition runs entirely offline from models bundled inside the app, so
photographing a page works in aeroplane mode. Your notes are yours.

FIVE SCRIPTS

Latin, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Each deck is set to the script
its pages are written in, because a recogniser pointed at the wrong script returns
confident nonsense rather than failing honestly. English, Hindi, Marathi, Sanskrit
and Nepali are all covered.

BUILT FOR REAL STUDYING

• Cards you forget come back within the same sitting, in minutes, not tomorrow
• One-step undo, because tapping Easy when you meant Again is the mistake
  everybody makes
• Cards you keep forgetting are set aside rather than repeated forever — a card
  that sticky needs rewriting, not more repetitions
• A two-week forecast, so a wall of reviews on Thursday is visible on Monday
• An honest retention figure over the last thirty days
• An optional daily reminder that stays quiet when you have nothing due, or have
  already studied that day. It is off until you turn it on.

YOUR COLLECTION, YOUR FILE

Export your whole collection — decks, cards and every scheduling detail — to a
single file you choose the location of. Restoring adds alongside what you have
rather than replacing it, so the wrong file costs you a few decks to delete
instead of everything you had.

ACCESSIBLE

Every control is labelled for TalkBack, every target is a real target, and the
layouts hold together at the largest system font sizes.

Abhyas means practice.
```

## Required assets still to produce

- **Feature graphic** — 1024 x 500 PNG or JPEG. Mandatory; the listing cannot be
  published without it.
- **Phone screenshots** — at least 2, between 320px and 3840px on the shorter side.
  Four to six is better: the deck list, the suggestions screen, a card being studied
  with the grade buttons showing their intervals, and the insights screen.
  Take them on a clean install with sensible sample decks, not test data.
- **App icon** — already present at `play-icon-512.png` (512 x 512).

## Data safety form answers

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.**
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Not
  applicable — no data is transmitted.
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
  Uninstalling removes everything; there is no server-side copy.

## Content rating

Expect **Everyone / PEGI 3**. The app has no user-generated content shared between
users, no ads, no purchases, and no communication features.

`privacy-policy.md` in this folder is the text. It must be published at a public URL
